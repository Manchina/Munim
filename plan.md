# Munim implementation plan

**Implementation status:** Application code, migrations, seeds and automated checks are now present. See [architecture and schema](docs/architecture.md), [endpoint contracts](docs/api.md), and [remaining pilot checks](docs/pilot-checklist.md). The checklist below remains the acceptance record: device/second-device checks, deployment PostgreSQL verification and the store pilot are not marked complete by source implementation alone.

Build the counter flow end to end with simulated text, then run the same commands through live speech on pilot devices. Use the Fastify API and PostgreSQL as the source of truth for sales, stock, restock requests, and Khata balances.

## Scope

- **First release** covers three responsive views, a persistent microphone bar, Hinglish and English commands, on-demand QR sharing, and an installable PWA with offline draft recovery.
- **Pilot follow-up** covers Telugu-English field testing, local unit conventions, and an opt-in wake-word trial if the device supports it and noise tests justify it. Fully offline checkout, automatic UPI reconciliation, and printing are outside the first release.

## Implementation checklist

- [x] **Scaffold the PWA and API.** Set up a React, Tailwind CSS, and TypeScript client and a Node.js, Fastify, and TypeScript API. Configure PostgreSQL and Drizzle migrations. Add environment templates, local setup and seed commands, and a repeatable type-check, test, and build workflow. Document the verified setup in the README.
- [x] **Model and seed the store data.** Add store-scoped products and aliases, inventory and stock movements, sales and line items, customers and Khata entries, and restock requests. Store money in integer paise, stock in integer grams or counts, and the checkout price on each sale line. Seed toor dal, chana dal, rice, sugar, atta, salt, cooking oil, tea, milk, Lifebuoy soap, bread, and biscuits with INR prices and thresholds. Set toor dal and bread just above their thresholds and seed Ramesh and Sita with itemized credit history. Leave Amul butter 500 g available as a demo request for an unstocked item.
- [x] **Build the command parser and simulator.** Return typed intents for adding items, checkout, restock requests, and Khata assignment, with distinct ambiguous and unsupported results. Recognize code-mixed connectors, spoken quantities, aliases such as `sabun`, unit phrases such as `aadha kilo`, and product sizes such as `500g`. Use an explicit conversion for `pav` when its meaning is configured for the store and ask for clarification on unfamiliar measures such as `ser`. Route text and runnable sample chips through the parser first. Show editable cart lines and support a quick voice correction or undo while a cart is open.
- [x] **Complete Live Counter and stock updates.** Show quantities, unit prices, line totals, and the INR cart total. Commit only on explicit checkout or a valid credit command. Revalidate prices and available stock on the server, prevent negative stock with transactional updates, and use idempotency keys so retries and double submissions cannot post a sale twice. Clear the cart after a successful commit. Keep low-stock badges visible at or below threshold and trigger one chime and spoken warning per item when committed stock crosses to that level.
- [x] **Build the Wholesale Restock List.** Persist requests for catalog and unstocked products, normalize equivalent names and sizes without merging distinct variants, and increment demand once per accepted command. Show request counts and use supplier groups where a supplier is assigned, with an unassigned group for the rest. Let the merchant mark requests procured and record received stock for catalog items.
- [x] **Build the Khata Credit Ledger.** Resolve the spoken name to a store customer, clarify ambiguous matches, and require confirmation above a store-configured credit threshold. Save the itemized sale, deduct stock, and post the timestamped debit in one transaction. Show pending balances and history. Have **Settle Payment** record a timestamped payment against the balance without changing inventory or deleting the original sale.
- [ ] **Add the on-demand QR flow.** Open **Share QR Bill** only when requested. Preview the itemized cart or completed sale. Issue a QR for a minimally scoped digital receipt link only for a finalized sale, with access limited to that receipt and no Khata details. For an unpaid cart, generate a separate amount-specific UPI payment QR when the store has a configured payee. Invalidate the payment QR if the cart changes and require merchant confirmation before recording payment. Verify both codes with a second device.
- [ ] **Add live speech and PWA behavior.** Use explicit start and stop controls, a visible listening state, live interim text, and deduplicated final transcripts. Keep typing and samples available for unsupported browsers, denied permissions, and recognition failures. Prevent the app's spoken warnings from being captured as commands. Add HTTPS deployment, a manifest, icons, and a service worker for the offline app shell. Preserve the active draft and available catalog locally, show offline status, and require reconnection and server validation before committing a transaction. Restrict pilot access to authenticated store users and scope reads and writes to their store. Keep audio and full transcripts out of application telemetry and explain browser speech processing before activation. [Web Speech API specification](https://webaudio.github.io/web-speech-api/#cloud-based-vs-on-device-implementations)
- [ ] **Verify the counter journey.** Test mixed-language cart parsing, unit and price rounding, checkout retries and concurrent sales, threshold equality and alert deduplication, duplicate restock requests, and credit posting and settlement. Test installation and UI on target phone and tablet browsers. Try microphone denial, a network drop at checkout, audio alerts while listening, and overlapping speech with fans and street noise. Confirm that unclear speech never posts a sale or changes a balance by itself.
- [ ] **Run and evaluate the pilot.** Recruit 20–30 stores for 30 days. Agree on rush-hour windows and each store's top-20 staple list before launch. Compare adoption and corrections during rush hours and compare credit disputes and stockouts with the prior month. Use sampled counter tallies to capture transactions that never enter Munim. Track command outcomes and recognition-to-preview latency without collecting raw speech.

## Pilot scorecard

| Metric              | Target                       | Measurement                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rush-hour adoption  | At least 75%                 | Share of eligible udhaar or loose-item transactions logged by voice without reverting to paper. Count overlapping order types once.                                                                                        |
| Hands-free accuracy | Below 10%                    | Share of voice entries requiring a manual edit, retry, or undo. Count an affected entry once even when it needs several corrections.                                                                                       |
| Revenue protection  | At least 50% fewer incidents | Compare unrecorded-credit disputes and unexpected stockouts of the agreed top-20 staples with the same store's prior-month records. Report the two measures separately and note missing baselines or zero baseline counts. |
